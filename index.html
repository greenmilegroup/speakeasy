The House Rules

A hidden destination for
food, music & celebrations.

Tucked into Ottawa's ByWard Market, Speakeasy Tapas Lounge is an elegant restaurant and live-music venue where the lights stay low and the night runs long.

Internationally inspired plates, craft cocktails poured with prohibition-era swagger, live performances every evening, and a candlelit room that feels like a secret worth keeping. Pull up a chair — this must be the place.
