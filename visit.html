Before you come

Good to Know

Getting here

In the heart of the ByWard Market. Street parking on York, and the Market parking garage is a two-minute walk.

Reservations

By phone. We hold tables for the evening and can seat walk-ins at the bar most nights.

Dietary needs

Vegetarian options on every menu. Tell us about allergies when you book and the kitchen will work around them.

Large groups

Six or more? Call ahead. For a private night, see private events, including the upstairs room.

Live music

Every night from 7 PM, no cover. Call for tonight's line-up.

Gift cards

Available in-house, a good answer for the person who has everything.
