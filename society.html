The terms, in plain words

How it works.

  1. Who it is for

    Anyone 19 or older. Membership is personal and carries your name; it cannot be lent or transferred.

  2. Shows

    General admission to ticketed shows on our stage for you and one guest, reserved by the day before, subject to availability. Two no-shows in a season and the seats go back to the list for that season.

  3. The big nights

    New Year's Eve, Valentine's Day and other special events are not included. Members get first access to those tickets, at a member price.

  4. Your table

    10% off food and drink for you and up to three guests when you dine with us, on your bill, before tax and gratuity. Not combined with other offers.

  5. Paying

    $50 a month, plus HST, charged automatically to the card you set up. You can cancel any time after your first six months; your membership runs to the end of the period you have paid for.

  6. Founding Members

    The first 100 members keep the $50 monthly rate for as long as they hold their membership without a break.

  7. Your card

    Lost or stolen cards are replaced once free of charge. Your card is ours to withdraw if the membership is misused.

  8. Changes

    We may change these terms or the benefits with thirty days' notice by email. If you do not like a change, you can cancel before it takes effect, whatever your start date.

  9. Questions

    Write to info@speakeasyottawa.com or call 613-241-6221.
